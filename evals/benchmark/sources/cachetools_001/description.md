With TTLCache(maxsize=2, ttl=2, timer=t), store a key at t=0. At t=2 the key is reported as gone by membership and iteration — 1 in cache is False and set(cache) does not contain it — yet len(cache) still counts it and cache.currsize still includes its size, and calling cache.expire() at t=2 returns an empty list. The entry is only cleaned up at t=3, one tick after its TTL ended. All views of the cache should agree on when an item expires.
